PRE-REGISTRATION: pose-stability dynamics, protocol v3 (item 13)     written 2026-10-25 17:04 IST, before any v3 trajectory was analysed
Why: in the first study the 2 ns screen (i) failed its own validity control (donepezil from the crystal pose exceeded the 3.0 A RMSD criterion in two of three runs), (ii) did not reproduce between
replicas (Spearman rho 0.11) and (iii) did not separate ten potent from ten inactive compounds (AUC 0.47).  Changes in v3, all fixed here:
  runs         donepezil (crystal pose) x 4 seeds (11-14); 15 potent (pChEMBL >= 8.0) and 15 inactive (<= 5.0) published compounds, property-matched (src/ad_v4_md_set.py), docked into 4EY7
               with the study's protocol, one 2.5 ns trajectory each (seed 31 / 32); same force field and procedure as the first study; the solute within 12 A of the ligand is saved for every frame (10 ps).
  metrics (computed offline from the saved frames, second half of production = last 1.25 ns, ligand fitted on the pocket C-alpha atoms within 8 A of the ligand at t = 0)
     M1  core RMSD: heavy atoms of the ligand's ring systems (flexible chains excluded)
     M2  contact persistence: number of the 12 key residues (Trp86, Trp286, Tyr124, Tyr337, Tyr341, Phe295, Phe297, Phe338, Asp74, Glu202, Ser203, His447) with a ligand heavy atom within 4.5 A of a
         residue heavy atom in >= 50 % of the second-half frames
     M3  Trp86 stacking fraction (ligand aromatic ring centroid within 5.5 A of the Trp86 ring centroid)
     (the first study's all-atom RMSD and centre-of-mass shift are also reported, as secondary)
VALIDITY CONTROL (must hold for the protocol to be used)
     V1  donepezil crystal pose, all four seeds: M1 <= 2.0 A, and M2 >= 5.
     V2  reproducibility: the standard deviation of M1 over the four donepezil seeds <= 0.5 A.
PRIMARY ENDPOINT   potent vs inactive, ROC-AUC (potent ranked as more stable: lower M1, higher M2, higher M3), 95 % bootstrap interval over compounds, Mann-Whitney p.
     The screen is called discriminating only if, for at least one of M1-M3, the AUC lower bound exceeds 0.5 and p < 0.05/3 (Bonferroni over three metrics), AND V1 and V2 hold.
If V1 or V2 fails, or no metric meets the endpoint, the dynamics are reported as non-discriminating and are not used in any claim.
